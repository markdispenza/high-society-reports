1 · The answer

Relabelling changes little. Of the 142 rows the old “citation” label produced across the six businesses, 99 survive the strict directory test. Everything that fell out was classified, not guessed: 8 social profiles, 5 job boards, 2 event pages, 6 news and media, 2 competitor sites, 5 scraper sites, 1 other non-directory, 7 country mirrors of one listing — the mirrors being 6 TripAdvisor country domains carrying one King of Budz listing. Under the clean label High Society has the second-most directory listings of the six (21); the two market leaders have 10 and 11. The number that matters is underneath: 505 of 677 directory × business cells (74.6%) are still undetermined — unmeasured, not absent.

“Citation” and “directory listing” are the same object measured two ways — backlink-proxied (what a link index can see) and page-verified (what a fetched page says) — so the label was never the variable; the method was.

"review everything we’ve done in the world of ‘finding citations’ and change the label to ‘finding directory listings’ and see if the data set changes dramatically or not. I want to find all directory listings for High Society new buffalo one more time... and their top 3 competitors directory listings."
— the question this report answers, verbatim

The three competitors are the top three by Share of Local Voice on “dispensary near me” in the 11×11 / 2.0 mi geo-grid scan of 8/29/2026 9:30 AM (competitor-audit/lf-dispensary-near-me-20260829.json): Dispo New Buffalo 42.35 · Lume Cannabis New Buffalo 41.18 · Vibe Cannabis Dispensary New Buffalo 35.29. High Society is 8th at 10.59. King of Budz (4th, 34.12) and Information Entropy (tied 8th with High Society on 10.59) also have full audit data and are carried as secondary columns throughout.

Statistical context, from PLAYBOOK.md §3–4: directory listings are null as a ranking factor in this dataset. Raw correlation with the residual is −0.231; hold review count constant and it collapses to −0.11, n.s., and among single-location businesses the correlation is 0.018 outright. That test proxied citations from the backlink index; the hand count in this report is page-verified and exists only for these six businesses. Fixing a listing is a hygiene and conversion decision, not a ranking lever.

Merge order, highest authority first

Two rules make the ladder safe. An undetermined never overrides a determination, whichever file it came from — so a walled page in the priority pass cannot erase a listing the index sweep proved. And the index sweep's undetermined:not-in-index stays undetermined: absence from Google's index is a reach limit, never absence from the directory. 11 · Rules learned today

Verbatim from citation-audit/RULES-TO-AVOID.md, rules 37–42 of 42.

  1. A business name is not an identity; a place ID is. Three MaryJane's stores share the Local Falcon name "MaryJane's". Matching on the name put the wrong store (Etobicoke, which beats its geography) into the playbook as the worst client for four hours; the real one was North York. Join every table on place_id and print the name only for humans.
  2. A city gate that passes on incidental rows is not a city gate. Leafbuyer's /listings/dispensaries/MI/new-buffalo is a statewide dump whose title is "Dispensaries Near Me in MI"; the resolver's city_mentions >= 3 check passed on three unrelated New Buffalo rows and produced two false not-listed verdicts before an agent re-measured the page. Prove the index is city-scoped (title, breadcrumb, or a majority of rows in the city) before any absence claim from it.
  3. "Citation" and "directory listing" are the same thing measured two ways; label the method, not the concept. The statistical tests proxied citations from the backlink index (linked, indexed listings only), the hand audit fetched pages. Calling one "citations" and the other "directory listings" invented a distinction the data never made and cost a full re-cut to show 99 of 142 rows survive the relabel. Say "backlink-proxied" or "page-verified".
  4. A merge that never ran is a pipeline bug, not a finding. The control-tested priority pass listed Lume on five general directories while out-lume/verdicts.json still said undetermined for all five; the two outputs sat side by side for a day. Every later stage must consume the union, and a verifier must fail when two verdict sources disagree without a recorded winner.
  5. Vendor UTM tags in a backlink pull are a ledger; read them. utm_source=Citations&utm_medium=Dispenza&utm_campaign=T1cite on 1,549 backlinks named every store the citation vendor built for, and New Buffalo was not among them. The backlink pull had been on disk for a day before anyone grouped url_to by city. Group by destination before spending on discovery.
  6. Bing's site: operator is dead for directory probing. site:yellowpages.com "Lume Cannabis" New Buffalo returned zero results on a listing known to exist; Google's returned ten unrelated Lume stores. $[redacted] to learn; do not spend more on operator queries on either engine.
