Read this before the numbers — five things the raw data does that will mislead you

  1. Review counts are frozen for the first 12 scans. Across the 11 consecutive pairs from 2025-07-29 to 2026-04-04, zero businesses changed review count in any pair. That is one cached snapshot repeated, not stability. Velocity is therefore measured only over 2026-05-02–2026-08-29 (6 scans, 3.91 months).
  2. The 2026-07-04 scan has 13 dead grid points that returned zero results. Its headline “rank 6” was posted on a partial scan. Every cliff figure here uses only the 72 points live in both scans.
  3. ARP is coverage-blind. It averages only the points where a business was found, so a business found at 18 of 85 points can outrank one found at 85. ATRP (not-found counted as 21) and SoLV are the honest metrics, and both are shown.
  4. Grid alignment verified, not assumed: all 18 scans use one identical ordered 85-point list, so point-by-point comparison is valid. The footprint is an 11×11 lattice missing 36 cells in the north-west (Lake Michigan) — so distance bands are not radially symmetric, and the far band skews south and east, inland.
  5. 3 scan-rows carry a null review count (Local Falcon returned no profile data). They are excluded from velocity rather than coerced to zero — coercing them manufactures a false −1,081/month.

Method, and what this does not show

Data. 18 cached Local Falcon scans of “dispensary near me” centred on the High Society pin, 11×11 at 2.0 mi, 85 points each, 2025-07-29 to 2026-08-29. Nothing was fetched; no credits were spent.

Censoring. Each grid point returns at most 20 results. A business absent from a point is scored 21 — derived from the data rather than assumed: Information Entropy at 2026-08-29 has found_in 18, ARP 6.94, ATRP 18.02, and (18×6.94 + 67x)/85 = 18.02 gives x = 21.00.

Bands. Live grid points sorted by distance to a pin and split into four near-equal groups by index. The like-for-like table uses High Society’s bands for every business, so the ground is identical.

This is observational. 18 scans of one keyword in one market, with review counts usable across only 3.91 months. Everything here is consistent with a prominence and relevance deficit; none of it establishes cause. The obvious confounders are untested: on-page and category relevance, GBP content, links and citations (audited separately), proximity weighting changes, and any Google ranking-system update inside the window.

Unverified. The reason the north-west lattice cells are absent is inferred from the geography (Lake Michigan); the data does not state it. The cause of the 12-scan review freeze is not established — it is diagnosed here as a stale cached snapshot because zero of ~30 businesses changed across 11 consecutive pairs, which no organic process produces. Whether House of Dank’s 3075-review fall is a listing merge, a purge or a data error is not determined; it is excluded from the robustness run either way.
