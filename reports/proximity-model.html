How much of local rank is just distance?

213,481 business-point observations · 121 Local Falcon grids · 29 markets · 3,423 businesses. Every earlier test regressed factors against raw rank, where "distance from grid centre" is an artifact — the grid is centred on the client's own pin. This model measures each business's distance to each grid point from its own pin, so nothing sits at zero by construction.

1 · Distance versus rank

Rank falls away steeply over the first two miles, then flattens. The upturn past roughly 64 miles is survivorship, not strength: at those distances only businesses good enough to still make a top-20 list appear at all, so the ones we can see rank well. 2 · The residual — who beats their geography

Expected rank is fitted inside each grid from that grid's own distance curve, so field size, keyword difficulty and grid radius are absorbed. Residual = observed rank − expected rank. Negative means the business outranks what its distance predicts.

5 · What is not proven here

  • Association, not cause. Nothing here identifies a mechanism. Every relationship above associates with the residual; none is shown to cause it, and the direction of causation between reviews and rank is not resolved here — ranking well brings footfall, which brings reviews.
  • The grid-centre premium is measured but not explained. Variant C halves it (-2.82 → -1.54) but 27 of 30 pairs still favour the centre. Part of it is likely real — the businesses we scan are the established operators in their own neighbourhood — and part may be residual geometry. The client adjustment subtracts the whole premium, which is the conservative choice.
  • Category filler. 51% of the businesses returned in these grids are not cannabis retailers. They are excluded from the leaderboards and re-tested separately in the factor table, but they remain in the distance curve, where they belong — they are genuinely occupying result slots.
  • Rank is right-censored at 20. Local Falcon returns at most 20 businesses per grid point, so a business ranked 21st is simply absent. 95 of 121 grids hit that ceiling. Variant B re-imputes absences at rank 21 to remove selection-on-appearance; both variants are reported side by side and are read together, not separately.
  • Clustering. Business-point observations repeat the same business across up to 121 points, so a naive p on 213,481 rows is meaningless. The distance result is therefore reported as an effect size plus a sign test across grids; factor tests are run on business-level aggregates.
  • Unequal grids. {"1":15,"2":57,"4":12,"8":12,"15":12,"100":13} reports by radius (mi), and one market (New Buffalo) contributes 37 of the 121 grids from a keyword/radius sweep. Fitting the expected-rank curve inside each grid contains this, but the pooled curve mixes scales.
  • Join coverage. Profile factors exist for 353 of 3,423 businesses; rendered-text metrics for 58. Factor tests run at those n, not the full field.
  • One vertical, one moment. Cannabis retail, grids scanned 2025-07-14 to 2026-08-29.
