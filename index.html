Proximity model — how much of local rank is distance

Across 213,481 observations, measures how much local rank is just distance, then tests which factors predict the leftover residual.

  • Proximity explains 47% of local rank (Spearman 0.69); competitive density is roughly the missing half.
  • Review count is the strongest residual predictor (rho -0.39); 12 apparent levers collapse into review mass.
